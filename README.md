# Command Control Protection Dogs (CCPD)

## Elite Family & Executive Protection Dogs

Official website: https://ccprotectiondogs.com

Command Control Protection Dogs specializes in breeding, developing, and training elite family and executive protection dogs.

Our development philosophy is built around three essential elements: genetics, environment, and training.

We believe exceptional protection dogs begin with strong genetic nerve, environmental stability, and professional development from an early age.

### Our Specialties
- Elite Family Protection Dogs
- Executive Protection Dogs
- German Shepherds
- Belgian Malinois
- Early Puppy Development
- Advanced Obedience
- Environmental Training
- Real-World Protection Training

### Our Philosophy

We work what we breed, and breed what we work.

Our objective is to develop balanced, socially stable, environmentally confident dogs capable of being trusted family companions and professionally trained protectors.
